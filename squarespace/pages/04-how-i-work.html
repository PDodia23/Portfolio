AI in my workflow

Faster, not lazier.

I use AI tools to move faster through the slow parts, and I check everything they produce against real users and real data. The home page video and the images on my About page are mine, made with Higgsfield AI.

Research synthesis

I use Claude and ChatGPT to sort interview notes and survey answers into first-draft themes, then check every theme against the raw quotes before I trust it.

Copy and content

I ask Claude or ChatGPT for lots of label and error-message options, shortlist the clearest few, then test them with real people.

Prototyping and code

I use Figma AI for quick visual exploration and Higgsfield for images and video, prototype in Figma, then build the parts that matter in HTML, CSS and JavaScript so people can test the real thing.
