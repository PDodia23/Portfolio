ABOUT

Driven by design,
grounded in code.

I've loved design since I was a child, and learning front-end development means I think about how things get built as well as how they look. My work starts with user research and testing, runs through a shared design system, and ends with a handoff engineers can build from without guessing. Outside design I'm usually halfway through a novel (currently The Night She Disappeared by Lisa Jewell) or planning the next trip.
