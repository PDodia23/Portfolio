Behind my home page film.

One clip from the experiments, and some of the versions that didn’t make the final cut.

I made the film on my home page in Higgsfield AI. Getting to the final version took a lot of tries: different rooms, different moments, cats in every one.

Higgsfield is temperamental. You have to be very precise with the prompt, and even then some versions came out completely wild. Here are a few of them. Hover over a clip to play it (on a phone, tap it).
