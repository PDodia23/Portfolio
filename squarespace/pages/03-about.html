About

Hi, I'm Pooja. I design it, build it and check it works.

I'm a UI/UX and product designer based in Kent. I've loved design since I was a child, and front-end development gave me a clear picture of how things actually get built. At Gravitai I design and develop the company's website and its design system, and I designed both versions of the site. Before that, as both designer and developer at Equerry Co Consulting, I designed client websites, built them myself in Squarespace and created a design system that made each site about 30% faster from design to live. I like the messy start of a project and the moment the numbers come back.
